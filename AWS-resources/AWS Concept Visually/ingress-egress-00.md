In AWS, ingress and egress are called traffic directions or rule types (inbound and outbound) rather than a "connection type." [1, 2]  
Understanding Ingress and Egress 

• Ingress: Incoming traffic that enters your network or resource. In AWS Security Groups, these are referred to as inbound rules. 
• Egress: Outgoing traffic that leaves your network or resource. In AWS Security Groups, these are referred to as outbound rules. [3, 4]
