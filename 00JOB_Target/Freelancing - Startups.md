## Best Platforms for Startup Tech & DevOps Jobs

* Wellfound: Formerly AngelList Talent, this is the top site specifically designed for startup hiring, offering direct access to early-stage and high-growth tech company listings with salary and equity transparency. [2] 
* Startup.jobs: Aggregates global and remote startup listings, featuring dedicated filters for cloud, infrastructure, and DevOps Jobs. [1] 
* [Instahyre](https://www.instahyre.com/devops-jobs/): An AI-based recruitment platform that matches your tech stack (Kubernetes, AWS, CI/CD) directly with recruiters at fast-growing product companies and startups. [3] 
* [JoinDevOps](https://jobs.joindevops.com/): A niche job board focusing explicitly on cloud engineering, SRE, and DevOps roles from entry-level to senior positions. [4] 
* [Indeed](https://in.indeed.com/q-devops-engineer-startup-jobs.html) & [Naukri](https://www.naukri.com/devops-jobs): General job engines where you can use search filters like "startup" or "early-stage" combined with keywords like "DevOps" or "SRE". [5, 6]
