# One more thing I think will increase your chances

You're currently focused on DevOps, but your background now also includes Python, Terraform, Kubernetes, Linux, and you're working through books like *Think Python* and *Data Science from Scratch*. That means you shouldn't restrict yourself to only "DevOps Engineer" openings.

Apply to roles where **60–70% of the requirements match your skills**. Many companies hire junior engineers who can grow into the role, and casting a wider but still relevant net often leads to more interview opportunities than waiting for the "perfect" job description.
